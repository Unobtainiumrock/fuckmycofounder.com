# Context

Glossary of domain terms. Definitions only — no implementation details.

## Terms

### Charge
The named category of cofounder offense a story or report is filed under
(e.g. "Selective Equity Amnesia"). The fixed taxonomy of charges is the
site's classification system for what went wrong.

### Case File
The rendered artifact of one incident report: charge, statement, quote,
translation, severity, and disposition, presented as a document.

### Cooked Quiz
The single-player diagnostic "How cooked are we?". Its subject is the
**partnership**, never the individual cofounder — questions ask about the
relationship the taker is inside of, and the result describes the state of
the partnership. (Decided over the alternative "Is your cofounder cooked?",
which diagnoses an absent person and reads as accusation.)

### Cooked Score
The Cooked Quiz's output: a measure of how endangered the partnership is.
Belongs to a partnership at a moment in time, not to a person.

### Risk Factor
A specific endangering condition of a partnership surfaced by the Cooked
Quiz (e.g. "no agreed exit terms"). Risk factors describe the partnership's
gaps, never a person's character. Each risk factor has a corresponding
treatment in the Toolkit.

### Toolkit
The prevention resources (checklists, scripts, calculators) that treat risk
factors. The quiz diagnoses; the Toolkit treats.

### Archetype
A fictional, illustrated recurring character personifying a failure pattern
(e.g. The Ghost). Archetypes belong to stories and the leaderboard — never
to quiz results, and never to real, identifiable people.

### Profile
The public record representing one individually identifiable, living adult with
substantiated professional participation in the startup ecosystem. A Profile
may exist without an Account and identifies its subject with only a canonical
professional name and profile photo.

### Profile Proposal
An Account-backed, nonpublic submission asking the platform to create a Profile
after search, eligibility, photo-provenance, duplicate, and moderation review.

### Profile Correction
An Account-backed proposal to change a Profile's canonical name or photo, or to
dispute its identity or eligibility. A Profile Correction does not itself
change the public Profile.

### Profile Merge
The reversible canonicalization of duplicate Profiles representing one person.
A Profile Merge preserves one canonical Profile while retaining the lineage
needed to resolve old references and undo an incorrect merge.

### Account
A person's lightweight sign-in identity for taking protected actions. An
Account is distinct from the Profile that may represent its owner publicly.

### Private Account Identity
The nonpublic authentication, contact, recovery, risk, and accountability
records associated with an Account. It is never a Public Byline or Profile.

### Profile Claim
The verified association between an Account and the Profile representing that
account holder.

### Relationship Claim
A reviewer's statement of the direct working relationship in which they
experienced the Profile subject, including its direction and approximate
period. Acceptance makes the claim eligible to support a Review but is not
proof of the relationship or Review.

### Relationship Verification
The derived state indicating that independent evidence supports the people,
direction, and approximate period in an accepted Relationship Claim. It does
not verify the Review's testimony, assessment, or conclusion.

### Review
A first-hand account of one working relationship with a Profile subject,
combining relationship context, a structured assessment, and qualitative
testimony.

### Review Revision
An immutable submitted version of a Review's relationship context, assessment,
testimony, attribution choice, and Exhibits. One approved Review Revision is the
current public version while prior versions remain restricted.

### Profile Subject Response
The one clearly labeled, separately moderated response a verified Profile
claimant may attach to a Review of that Profile. It does not edit, replace, or
control the Review.

### Public Byline
The display name and optional photo an Account uses for named participation. A
Public Byline is not automatically a Profile.

### Anonymous Attribution
The public presentation of an Account-backed Review as **Anonymous reviewer**
without a Public Byline, Profile link, or stable identity across Reviews. The
Account association remains private to the platform.

### Block
An Account's unilateral safety boundary with another Account. A Block prevents
direct interaction and targeted discovery but is not a global content takedown.

### Report
An Account-backed request for the platform to evaluate a specific object or
behavior under its rules. A Report opens or joins a Moderation Case; it is not
itself a verdict.

### Moderation Case
The platform record used to evaluate one or more related Reports, evidence,
decisions, notices, and Appeals without exposing confidential participants.

### Enforcement Action
A scoped consequence applied to an object, Account, or Profile Claim after a
policy decision. It is distinct from the Moderation Case that produced it.

### Appeal
An affected Account's request to review an eligible Enforcement Action or
Profile Claim decision using preserved context and any new information.

### Post
A named publication by an Account. A Post has no Relationship Claim or Review
Assessment and is not a substitute for a Review of a Profile subject.

### Post Revision
An immutable submitted version of a Post's text, links, and images. One eligible
Post Revision is the current public version while prior versions remain
restricted.

### Comment
An Account-backed response attached to a Review, Post, or another Comment. A
Comment adds discussion without changing the object it responds to.

### Comment Thread
The discussion attached to one Review or Post. It contains top-level Comments
and one visible reply level; a Profile Subject Response remains outside the
Comment Thread.

### Comment Revision
An immutable submitted version of a Comment's text and links. One eligible
Comment Revision is the current public version while prior versions remain
restricted.

### Thread Lock
A moderation state that prevents new Comments and replies in one Comment Thread
without preventing reports, Appeals, or authors from editing or deleting their
existing Comments.

### Slow Mode
A temporary moderation state that limits how frequently each eligible Account
may publish in one Comment Thread.

### Review Assessment
The structured startup-collaboration scores recorded as part of a Review. An
assessment describes capabilities observed in one working relationship, not
the Profile subject's permanent character or an objective prediction of
success.

### Profile Aggregate
The derived summary of currently eligible Review Assessments on one
Profile. It describes what a sufficient set of reviewers reported, with
relationship and evidence context; it is not authored Profile identity, a
universal Founder Score, or a prediction of success.

### Feed
A ranked view of published Reviews and Posts. A Feed item is a presentation of
one of those objects, not a separate content object.

### Review Vote
An Account's upvote or downvote on whether a Review is useful, specific, and
worth seeing. It evaluates the Review, not the Profile subject.

### Verdict Vote
An Account's optional poll response about the situation described by a Review.
It is distinct from a Review Vote and is never a Profile score.

### Award Credit
The non-stackable right an eligible Account receives at each weekly refresh to
give one Review Award. Its balance is at most one, and an unused credit expires
at the next refresh.

### Review Award
A scarce endorsement created when an Account spends its Award Credit on a
Review. A Review may receive Awards from multiple Accounts.

### Notification
An Account-private, recipient-specific projection of an eligible product event.
It contains only the canonical source context and actions safe for that recipient.

### Exhibit
An optional image attached to a Review to help illustrate the first-hand
account. An Exhibit is moderated context, not automatic verification of the
Review or Relationship Claim.

### Share Clip
A platform-rendered visual excerpt of a Profile, Review, or Comment made only
from its current eligible public projection. A Share Clip preserves exact source
words, mandatory context, attribution, and a Canonical Share Link; presentation
choices cannot rewrite its source.

### Canonical Share Link
The stable first-party HTTPS link for a currently shareable Profile, Review,
Post, Comment, or Profile Subject Response. It identifies the source rather than
the sharer or recipient and resolves according to the source's current public
lifecycle; a Profile Subject Response opens inside its Review context.

### Direct Message
A private message sent between Accounts. A Direct Message is never addressed
to an unclaimed Profile or used to reveal an anonymous Review author.

### Follow
An Account-private, one-way subscription to a canonical Profile's eligible public
activity. A Follow is not a mutual relationship, Relationship Claim, message
permission, endorsement, or public credibility signal.

### Message Identity
The authorized public identity an Account presents in a message request or Direct
Message conversation: either its current Public Byline or a Profile controlled by
its current verified Profile Claim. It never uses Private Account Identity or
Anonymous Attribution.

### Message Request
One bounded first-contact message from an eligible Account to another Account that
has enabled requests through a Message Identity. Acceptance opens a Direct Message
conversation; the request itself is not a Connection, Follow, or consent to other
contact.

### Open To Status
An Account-owned, self-declared signal that its holder is open to hooking up,
dating, or a relationship during one fixed 14-day window. It projects only
through the Account's current verified claimed Profile to another eligible
active participant in an ordinary Profile context and can be withdrawn at any
time; it is not a public Profile field or consent to contact or activity.

### Adult Eligibility Assertion
A nonpublic, purpose-limited result establishing whether an Account holder meets
Open To's 18+ threshold. It does not expose or establish the person's exact age,
birth date, assurance evidence, Public Byline, or Profile identity.
