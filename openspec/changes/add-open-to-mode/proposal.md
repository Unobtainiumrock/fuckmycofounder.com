## Why

Open To can add a candid adult connection layer to the startup network, but public availability, irreversible activation, weak age gates, or a dating-discovery directory would turn professional Profiles into harassment and outing surfaces. It must remain a later, isolated, mutually visible mode whose participation and messaging permissions can be withdrawn immediately.

## What Changes

- Add an adults-only Open To Status for an eligible Account with a verified Profile Claim and a separate privacy-preserving 18+ eligibility result; no Public Byline is required.
- Let a participant choose one or more of **Hook up**, **Date**, and **Relationship** for one fixed 14-day window.
- Show the status only when two currently active participants encounter each other through an existing ordinary Profile surface. Open To does not add a directory, search filter, ranking signal, recommendation pool, swipe, Like, Match, preference filter, or location discovery.
- Replace the proposed two-week lock-in with immediate withdrawal. Leaving ends visibility now and prevents reactivation until the original window closes; changing intentions never extends expiry.
- Keep receiving Open To introductions as a second, default-off consent. Eligible requests reuse Message Request quotas, lifecycle, safety controls, and Direct Message acceptance.
- Add Open To-specific blocking, reporting, underage, coercion, sexual-harassment, outing, retaliation, retention, notification, and non-leakage behavior.
- Keep the capability disabled by default until age assurance, privacy and child-safety review, counsel, moderation operations, abuse testing, and deployed non-leakage checks pass.

Explicitly out: first-release enablement, whole-pool discovery, dating recommendations, matches, sexual-orientation or target preferences, precise or coarse location, distance, media, contact exchange, sexual-content permission, public status, Feed or reputation integration, Share Clips, and screenshot-prevention or recall claims.

## Impact

- Adds capability `open-to` and extends the existing Message Request entry contract.
- Depends on Accounts, verified Profile Claims, published Profiles, centralized policy, Blocks, Reports, notifications, Message Requests, and Direct Messages.
- Main risks are minors evading assurance, sensitive-intention disclosure, workplace power abuse, stalking, outing, compromised Accounts, rejected-introduction retaliation, and screenshots outside platform control.
