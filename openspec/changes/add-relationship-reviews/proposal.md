## Why

Profiles become useful when people who actually worked with the subject can add credible context. A public Review of an identifiable person also creates retaliation, defamation, privacy, anonymous-author leakage, and coordinated-abuse risks. The product therefore needs one contract that makes first-hand Relationship Claims, accountable attribution, observable assessments, testimony, Exhibits, moderation, and correction inseparable.

## What Changes

- Add directional Relationship Claims for cofounders or partners, manager/report relationships, peers, investor/advisor relationships, and external collaborators, with approximate period, duration, attestation, acceptance, optional private evidence, and a distinct verified state.
- Require an accepted Relationship Claim before a Review can publish and permit only one active Review for the same reviewer, Profile, and continuous relationship period.
- Add signed-out local drafting followed by Account-backed submission, first-hand attestation, bounded qualitative prompts, named or publicly anonymous attribution, pre-publication moderation, immutable revisions, withdrawal, disputes, limitations, removal, and appeal hooks.
- Let a published named Review permanently step down to **Anonymous reviewer**; do not allow an anonymously published Review to reveal its author later at launch.
- Add optional, moderated Exhibits with safe image processing, redaction, provenance, restricted originals, and no claim-verification effect.
- Add the launch Review Assessment: **LARP**, **Domain Expertise**, **On Time**, **Taste**, **GTM**, **Charisma**, and **Run it back**, with exposure-aware presentation, behavioral anchors, and no universal Founder Score.
- Notify a verified Profile claimant only after publication, then permit correction or dispute requests and one separately moderated, clearly labeled Profile Subject Response without giving the subject editorial or takedown control.

Explicitly out: Comments, Review Votes, Verdict Votes, Awards, profile aggregates, Feed ranking, sharing, Direct Messages, and ordinary Posts.

## Impact

- Adds capability specs `relationship-claims`, `reviews`, and `review-assessments`; depends on the validated Account, Public Byline, trust-safety, Profile, and Profile-search contracts.
- Introduces relationship-policy, immutable Review-revision, attribution-projection, moderation, and Exhibit-processing seams.
- Main risks are fabricated relationships, retaliatory Reviews, confidential evidence, doxxing, anonymous leakage, coercive attribution changes, brigading, and scores presented as objective truth. Specialist legal review remains a pre-launch gate for defamation, privacy, evidentiary, notice, retention, and jurisdiction-specific takedown policy.
